# Credits

## Kanji data

`kanji.json` is derived from the **KANJIDIC2** dictionary file, the property of
the [Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group](https://www.edrdg.org/),
and used in conformance with the group's
[licence](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html).

KANJIDIC2 is licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
`kanji.json` is a derivative work — it selects the 2136 jōyō kanji, keeps the
English meanings (capped at four per kanji) along with the `ja_on` and `ja_kun`
readings, and reorders them — and is therefore distributed under the same
CC BY-SA 4.0 license.

Changes made are entirely contained in `tools/build-deck.mjs`.
