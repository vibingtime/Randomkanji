# Credits

`kanji.json` is generated by `tools/build-deck.mjs` from the three sources
below. It is a combined derivative work and carries all of their terms.

## Example sentences — Tatoeba

The example sentences are from the [Tatoeba Project](https://tatoeba.org/),
used here via the Japanese side of the
[OPUS Tatoeba en-ja corpus](https://opus.nlpl.eu/Tatoeba/). Many of them
originate in the Tanaka Corpus.

Tatoeba sentence data is licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France (CC BY 2.0 FR)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/).
Sentences are reproduced verbatim except that the four kanji whose glyphs
changed in the 2010 jōyō revision are displayed in their current forms
(填→塡, 頬→頰, 叱→𠮟, 剥→剝).

## Kanji list and readings — KANJIDIC2

The jōyō kanji list, the grade and frequency data used to order the deck, and
the reading list used to validate generated furigana come from **KANJIDIC2**,
the property of the [Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group](https://www.edrdg.org/),
used in conformance with the group's
[licence](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html).

KANJIDIC2 is licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

## Word segmentation and readings — IPADIC via kuromoji

Furigana is generated at build time by [kuromoji](https://github.com/takuyaa/kuromoji.js)
(Apache License 2.0) using the IPADIC dictionary, which supplies the word
boundaries and readings that the furigana is fitted from. IPADIC is
copyright 2003 Nara Institute of Science and Technology and is distributed
under its own BSD-style licence, reproduced in
`node_modules/kuromoji/NOTICE.md` when kuromoji is installed.

Fallback vocabulary for the kanji with no corpus sentence is taken from the
same dictionary.
