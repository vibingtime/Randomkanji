How it works
  1. A card shows one kanji on its own. Try to read it, then tap the card to turn it over.
  2. The back is one real sentence using that kanji. The word being tested is underlined, and its meaning is the line underneath.
  3. Grade yourself honestly: Again if you could not read it, Got it if you could.
  4. Small kana appear above the kanji you have not been taught yet, and always above the one being tested. They thin out as you progress.
  5. Each Got it stretches the gap before you see that kanji again: 1 day, then 3, then a week, 3 weeks, 2 months, 6 months. Again shortens it right back and re-asks you this session.
  6. Twenty new kanji a day to begin with; reviews are never capped. There are 2136 in all — every kanji taught in Japanese schools.

Progress is saved on this device only. It is never uploaded, and it does not follow you to another phone or browser.
